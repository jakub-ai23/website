Why a seminar is not enough here

A seminar shows you what AI can do. Here we set it up.

In one-to-one training you get eight hours of it. That is why it costs more than a seat in a group, and why it comes out cheaper in the end than three seminars after which nothing changes in your day.

How it runs

Install, work, correct.

One single long day fills you up and then leaves you on your own. In sport, nobody trains once for eight hours and then never again either. That is why I work in stages.

01

Intro call, free

20 minutes. What do you do every day, where do you lose time, what are you already working with. After that I know whether one-to-one training fits your task. If it does not, I say so and recommend something else.

02

The installation

The long block, on site or online. We set your tools up around your real tasks: projects, system prompts, reusable routines. You do the typing, I sit beside you and correct.

03

The correction

Two shorter online appointments, one to two weeks apart. The really good questions come up in the working day. On the training day nobody knows them yet.

Frequently asked questions

Before you ask.

Do I need prior knowledge?

No. I regularly work with people who cannot even sign in at the start, and in the same room with people who already write their own system prompts. That is exactly what the one-to-one format is for: there is no pace you have to keep up with.

Which tool do we learn?

The one you actually work with. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot. The foundations are the same across all of them, and those foundations are the real content. If your organisation only permits Copilot, we do Copilot.

Do I get a certificate for my employer?

Yes, you receive a personalised certificate of attendance for the training completed, including the content on data protection and the EU AI Act. For bookings through a further-education institute, that institute issues the certificate.

Online or on site?

Both. Online is more flexible and cheaper, because there are no travel costs. On site is stronger when we work at your actual workstation and on your systems. The most common arrangement: one appointment on site, then the follow-up online.

And if it does not help?

Then tell me in the first hour and we stop. I have no interest in customers who stay seated out of politeness.
