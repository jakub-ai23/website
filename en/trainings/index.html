How it works

From intro call to follow-up

  1. Intro call

    We discuss where AI should help in your organisation and what impact you expect from it.

  2. Proposal

    You then receive a written proposal.

  3. Preparation

    I build the exercises and the learning page around the tasks of your department and the tool your organisation uses.

  4. Training

    Your team works on a split screen: on the left the learning page with all exercises and prompts to copy, on the right the AI tool. Core tasks for everyone, bonus tasks for those who finish early.

  5. Follow-up

    After the training I keep supporting you, with e-learning and exercises, so that what your team has learned sticks in everyday work.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which training suits my team?

We work that out in the intro call. As a rule of thumb: if people do not work much with AI yet, start with “AI at your desk” or the training for your own tool. HR, sales and marketing get a training with examples from their department.

We have Copilot. Do we still need ChatGPT?

No. We train with the tool your team uses: Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

My people are at very different levels. Does that work in one group?

Yes. Every learning page has core tasks for everyone, with prompts to copy, and bonus tasks for those who finish early. Nobody waits, nobody is left behind.

How many people per training?

Ideally 8 to 12 people, which leaves time to guide each person on their own task. That said, we agree the exact group size with you.

How long does a training last?

As a rule, one hands-on day. Two half-days are also possible.

What does a training cost?

That depends on format, duration and location. After a short intro call, you will receive a written proposal.

May my employees use real data?

Yes, if your data is in a secure environment. Then we work directly with your data on your computers. There is also a simple rule for what may and may not go into the AI, which your team applies in daily work afterwards.

Do participants get a certificate for the EU AI Act?

Yes. Each person receives a certificate of attendance covering data protection and the EU AI Act. This lets you document AI literacy under Article 4, which has applied since 2 February 2025.

Does the training take place on site or online?

At your premises or live online.

Which languages are the trainings held in?

Every training is available in German or in English.
