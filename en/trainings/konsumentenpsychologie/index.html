Why psychology comes before the prompt

Why don't people buy, even when the offer is right? The answer often lies not in the product, but in unconscious psychological barriers that stand between your message and your customers' decision. This resistance works automatically, and your customers usually cannot name it themselves.

AI acts as an amplifier of your communication, for better and for worse. If you understand the psychology behind it, you also see what to watch out for when generating content with AI.

You will not learn ready-made scripts or tricks. You learn the psychological system behind them, using concrete examples from B2B and B2C. The principles apply everywhere, on landing pages as well as in sales conversations, newsletters or pitches.

The content

Foundation

How people really decide

Why rational arguments are often not enough, what role unconscious processes play in buying decisions and how System 1 and System 2 work together in marketing.

Core

Four barriers and five motivators

What kinds of psychological resistance stop customers from acting, how you recognise each barrier and remove it deliberately, with practical examples from different industries. Plus the five motivators.

One example: uncertainty. If it is unclear what happens after the decision, customers would rather not decide at all. You will learn the other barriers and the motivators in the training.

AI

Psychology and AI

How AI tools amplify your communication, what you need to watch out for when you use AI for content, and how the psychological foundation becomes a quality filter. Plus: guiding AI so that copy is written consistently to frameworks such as SLAY, and having it generate hook variants.

Practice

Exercises and tools to take away

Analysing your own projects and campaigns, in individual and group work with immediate feedback. Plus a universal analysis framework and a checklist for barrier analysis that you can use again and again.
