- **Dates** in training cards are DD.MM.YYYY (25.06.2026). For EN: render as **25 June 2026**, **6 July 2026**, **14-15 July 2026** (day-month-year, EU business standard, no ordinal superscript). "Next date: 25 June 2026".
- **Currency:** none on page.
- **lang attribute:** `<html lang="de">` → `<html lang="en">`.
- **og:locale:** `de_AT` → `en_GB` (neutral EU business, en_GB is the safe spelling-neutral-leaning choice). og:url / canonical → keep domain but note EN path (`/en/`) — orchestrator sets these.
- **Schema (ld+json):** translate `description`, `jobTitle`, `serviceType`, `areaServed.description` to English; set `addressLocality` to "Vienna"; `name` → "Jakub Popluhar - AI Training & Consumer Psychology". Keep addressCountry "AT", coords, URLs.
- **Spelling convention:** -ise/-isation (personalise, organised, specialise), "programme" (not program), "behaviour", "cooperation". Avoid US "-ize". Avoid UK slang. This is the neutral-EU lean.
