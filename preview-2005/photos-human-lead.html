Hill Digital / Human in the Lead
Master Claim · Photography

Human in the Lead. AI in the background.

Twelve photos chosen specifically to embody the master claim — humans in the foreground (face, hands, decision), technology secondary, supportive, often blurred or peripheral. The composition is the message.

Composition rule. The human is in focus, in colour-of-attention, in command. The screen / device / dashboard is present but secondary — softened by depth-of-field, framed at the edge, or behind a thoughtful gaze. Never the reverse: never a human dwarfed by glowing UI. We work with technology, we don't worship it.
