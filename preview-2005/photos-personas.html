Persona 01

Heads of HR · HR Directors

Mostly women, 38–55, professional but warm. Authority that comes from listening, not from posturing. They want a vendor who respects their craft, not one who promises to "disrupt HR".

Strong eye contact, calm. The "she runs HR for 800 people" feeling.
1.2 · Open posture, listening
Mid-conversation. Warmth without softness — exactly the HR-leader register.
1.3 · Side-light portrait
Editorial, thoughtful. Works as hero with right-side negative space for headline.
1.4 · Mid-career, composed
Mature, sophisticated. The face of someone whose first hire was 20 years ago.
Persona 02

HR Business Partners · HR Operations

28–45, the ones who actually run the routines that Hill Digital takes off their plate. They want visible relief and a tool that respects their judgement. Often shown as small teams, real interaction, not single-portrait.

2.1 · Team working, daylight
Three people at a table, mid-conversation. The "HRBP huddle" moment.
2.2 · Standing, briefing
Standing handover or check-in — the operational tempo of HR.
2.3 · Interview / selection moment
Two people, structured conversation across a table. Reads as "good interview".
2.4 · Workshop wall, process
Sticky notes, planning, methodology visible. Anchors "method before tool".
Persona 03

Executive Assistants · Chief of Staff

28–48, the people who actually make C-level decisions executable. They are organised, well-informed, and read everything before their boss does. They evaluate vendors first; the CEO meets us only if they recommend us.

3.1 · Focused at desk
Concentrated, organised. The "she has the boss's full schedule in her head" moment.
3.2 · On the phone, listening
Gatekeeper energy. Polite but unmistakably the deciding voice in the room.
3.3 · Taking notes, structured
Notebook, pen, considered. The Chief-of-Staff aesthetic.
3.4 · Walking briefing
In motion, conversation in transit. Captures the tempo of CoS work.
Persona 04

C-Level · CHRO, CEO, COO

45–65, decision-makers. We need them to read three sentences and understand: "this is serious, EU-grounded, methodologically credible." Imagery: gravitas without stiffness, real decision moments, no American-style smiles.

4.1 · Executive meeting, eye contact
Two senior people, decision moment. Quiet, considered, not theatrical.
4.2 · Senior man, wisdom
Older executive, weathered authority. Reads as "decades of experience".
4.3 · CEO portrait, considered
Sober, no power-pose. The DACH-CEO register: result-focused, not telegenic.
4.4 · Senior, side-light
Editorial portrait, contemplative. Pairs with a heritage / position-paper context.
