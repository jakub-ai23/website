Onboarding

AI in onboarding.
Faster productivity. Stronger retention.

The first 90 days decide whether a new hire turns into a long-term partnership. We help companies with AI-supported onboarding and the right trainings, so new team members become productive faster and feel properly integrated. Whether you're a Head of HR in a mid-sized company, a Managing Director with an HR team, or a business owner without an HR department of your own.

The picture in Austria

Onboarding today. What has changed.

Three points that come up in every intro call.

01
Early turnover

A failed onboarding costs one annual salary.

Early resignations are more common in the Austrian mid-market than most companies care to admit. Every early exit costs time, recruiting budget, and trust. That makes structured onboarding one of the most effective HR measures there is.

02
SMB reality

In companies without an HR team, onboarding often happens on the side.

A dedicated HR function often doesn't exist. New team members get the keys, a brief introduction, and a list of passwords. A lot of it is improvised, and that is exactly where AI can help quickly.

03
EU AI Act

New team members today also learn how to work with AI.

The EU AI Act makes AI competence mandatory. Companies that build it in during onboarding create clarity from day one, instead of paying to fix it later. New team members learn not only processes and tools, but also how to use AI responsibly.
