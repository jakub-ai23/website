What stays. What changes.

Methodology is what stays.

Three generations of Hill understood one thing: methodology comes before the tool. Assessment logic, structured interviews, a proprietary audit method — grown over five decades.

What changes: HR professionals no longer have to do the routine work themselves. What doesn't: every good decision still begins with a human who listens and judges.

Five decades. One line.

Three generations of Hill — and the step to Hill Digital.

1975

Founded in Vienna

Hill Personalberatung starts as a family-owned firm with the ambition to ground people decisions in method rather than gut feel.

1990s

Going international

Expansion into CEE markets. Hill establishes itself as one of the most respected executive search firms in the German-speaking region, with a distinct methodology school.

2010s

Hill Woltron Management Partner

Merger into Hill Woltron Management Partner GmbH. Sharpening the profile on executive search, management audits and people development — methodologically rigorous, personally tangible.

2025/26

Hill Digital

Three generations of methodology meet AI and automation. Hill Digital turns 50 years of assessment logic into a sovereign, EU-compliant HR operating system — for DACH SMEs and corporates.

Our philosophy

Others start with tech. We start with three generations of Hill recruiting methodology since 1975 — giving HR professionals their time back for what matters: critical thinking and real relationships.

Our systems take over the bulk of the routine work. Your HR teams get 20 to 30 percent of their time back — for exactly the tasks no algorithm will ever take on.

Three principles

How we measure Hill Digital.

Human in the Lead

Decisions about people are made by people. AI prepares, documents, suggests — and steps back where it matters.

Sovereign by EU standard

Method before tool
