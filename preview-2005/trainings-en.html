Before every in-house training

Listen first, then build.

We don't only talk to the leadership level, we also talk to the people who will use the tools every day. That way we understand where work piles up, which processes slow you down and where targeted improvements make the biggest difference. The workshop is built on that.

A training succeeds when both sides benefit: leaders see concrete value, and team members experience that AI actually makes their daily work easier.

Training 01, for decision-makers EN on request

AI for leaders

For C-level, managing directors and heads of department. This is not about tool operation, it's about the question of how AI may create impact in your organisation and where lines are drawn. Strategy, governance, risk, posture.

  • Audience: CEOs, managing directors, board members, heads of department, supervisory board members.
  • Format: compact half-day or full-day seminar. Confidential, small group, cross-industry possible.
  • Topics: strategic positioning of AI in the company, EU AI Act and compliance at board level, investment decisions, risk classification, responsibility and liability.
  • We work on your real decision questions, not on generic use cases.
  • Includes certificate as proof of AI competence under Article 4 EU AI Act.

Training 02, for decision-makers EN on request

AI for managers

For team and department leads. You are the layer between strategy and day-to-day operations. We show where AI actually takes load off your team, how to lead adoption inside the team, and where the decision must stay with the human.

  • Audience: team leads, department heads, middle management, project leads.
  • Format: full-day seminar or two half-days. Maximum 12 participants, case-based.
  • Topics: AI in operational leadership, preparing briefings and meetings, reporting, people management and feedback with AI as a tool, running pilot projects in the team.
  • We address the honest questions: what does AI take off my plate, what must it not take over, how do I lead a team through adoption.
  • Includes certificate as proof of AI competence under Article 4 EU AI Act.
