Hill Heritage
50+
Years of HR expertise. Three generations. One digital answer.
What stays. What changes.

Expertise is what stays.

Three generations of Hill have understood one thing: expertise comes before tool. Assessment logic, structured interviews, our own audit methodology, grown over five decades.

What changes: HR professionals no longer have to do the routine work themselves. What doesn't change: at the start of every good decision stands a person who listens and judges.

Five decades. One line.

Three generations of Hill, the step to Hill Digital.

1975

Founded in Vienna

Hill HR Consulting opens as a family-run HR consultancy with the ambition to back personnel decisions with method, rather than gut feel.

1990s

Internationalisation

Expansion into Central European markets. Hill establishes itself as one of the most distinctive HR consultancies in the German-speaking region, with its own expertise.

2010s

Hill Woltron Management Partner

Merger into Hill Woltron Management Partner GmbH. Sharpening the profile around executive search, management audits and people development, methodically rigorous, personally tangible.

2025/26

Hill Digital

Deep HR assessment logic meets AI and automation. Hill Digital turns that into Recruiting, Onboarding and AI Training modules. Sovereign, EU-compliant, international.

Our ecosystem

Small at the core. Strong in our network.

Behind Hill Digital sit training partners, consulting know-how, the locations of the Hill Group, and a live pilot lab for new applications. Four pillars that carry us.

01 · Training distribution

Where our trainings become visible

ARS Akademie

Jakub Popluhar is listed as a trainer with ARS Akademie. There is no formal partnership between Hill Digital and ARS. What there is: an established distribution touchpoint in the Austrian further-education market, through which Hill Digital trainings are also accessible outside the in-house format.

02 · Strategy and consulting partners

Who builds the plans with us

InnovaticGroup

Strategic consulting partner with depth in AI agent architectures. Karl Pichler, CEO of InnovaticGroup, was door-opener and mentor in the founding phase. InnovaticGroup supports Hill Digital strategically and brings technical depth on complex implementation questions.

03 · Hill Group sister locations

Who works with us regionally and internationally

Hill Munich, Hill Vienna, Hill Warsaw

Hill Digital is a member of the Hill Group. Three locations, three languages, three legal jurisdictions, three labour markets first-hand. Expertise flows between the locations, lessons learned from DACH and Central Europe land in our workflows.

04 · Pilot and lab partners

Where we learn, before we deliver

Hill Woltron Management Partner GmbH

50 years of recruiting experience meet new AI tools, not in demo mode, but in real daily work. Hill Woltron is the first Living Lab of Hill Digital: a place where we test new applications on ourselves first, before we recommend them. What works, we develop further. What doesn't, we drop without hesitation.
